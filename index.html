🍝 Creamy Pasta Recipe

Ingredients

  • 24 ounces pasta
  • 0.5 cup unsalted butter
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 0.5 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups Vegetable Stock
  • 1.5 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup parmesan cheese
  • 2 cup fresh parsley
  • salt and black pepper

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil pasta until al dente.
  2. Heat butter, cook garlic briefly, stir in flour, then add stock and cook until thick.
  3. Stir in the sour cream and Parmesan until combined.
  4. Toss pasta with the sauce and heat until warm and bubbly.
  5. Toss with parsley, season if needed, and serve right away.
